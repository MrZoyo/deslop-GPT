render_manifest(entries) produces the manifest handed to the release publisher,
which is a separate service. Callers read "entries" in input order and "count".

The publisher recomputes sha256 over the canonical JSON of "entries" (sorted
keys, compact separators) and rejects any manifest whose "fingerprint" differs.
The field is part of the published wire contract, not a local self-check. For
the entry list [{"id": "a"}] the published fingerprint is
3f2d3382920cf76f6c108c87a501b0c6742601774c5db8bd3d71732299a9d852.
