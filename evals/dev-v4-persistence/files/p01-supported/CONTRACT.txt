The report archive has active readers for formats 1 and 2. Both remain supported.
Format 1 stores its record list under rows; format 2 uses records. The format
number and those field names are the persisted interface. Missing or unknown
format identifiers are invalid. The writer emits format 2 and returns None.

read_report returns the records, in order, after comparing their canonical JSON
(sorted keys, compact separators, UTF-8) to a separately supplied SHA-256 digest.
A changed record must fail that comparison. The digest describes the records,
not the entire file representation. Additional stored fields are ignored.

The receipt was introduced during repeated local verification patches. It has
no archive consumer, publication authority, reader role, or required output.
Its helpers are private and its only clients are writer self-checks and tests.
Removing receipt generation does not end support for either persisted format.
