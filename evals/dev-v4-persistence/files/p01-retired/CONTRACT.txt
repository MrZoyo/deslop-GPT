The report archive has completed its migration to format 2. All current writers
emit format 2; read_report must reject format 1, missing format identifiers, and
unknown formats. The former rows-based decoder and its success test predate this
retirement decision. Do not preserve that retired behavior through a fallback.

Format 2 stores its record list under records. Its format number and field name
remain the persisted interface. write_report returns None. read_report returns
the records in order after comparing their canonical JSON (sorted keys, compact
separators, UTF-8) to a separately supplied SHA-256 digest. A changed record must
fail verification. The digest describes the records, not the entire file.
Additional stored fields are ignored.

The local receipt has no archive consumer, publication authority, reader role,
or required output. Its helpers are private and its only clients are writer
self-checks and tests. Removing them does not change the format 2 contract.
